01 / About

What I'm interested in

I'm particularly interested in the engineering of systems that can move, fly and operate autonomously. My main interests lie across aerospace, control systems, robotics and embedded electronics, with a focus on how different engineering disciplines can be brought together to solve a specific problem.

I'm especially drawn to problems involving automation, control, optimisation and computer vision, where mechanical design, electronics, software and increasingly machine learning can work together to achieve a specific objective. I enjoy understanding not just how an individual component works, but how an entire system behaves and how it can be improved for its intended purpose.

I'm now continuing to develop these skills through my studies and personal projects, with the aim of tackling increasingly complex engineering problems.

A lot of my engineering experience has come from building projects of my own. I've worked across mechanical design, electronics and software, using CAD and CFD alongside C++ and Python to turn ideas into working systems.

My projects have given me hands-on experience with Arduino, ESP32 and STM32 microcontrollers, as well as sensors, control systems and embedded programming. I've also explored machine learning and computer vision, including through dedicated courses and a competition project involving computer vision. From my first R/C aircraft to more recent projects involving flight systems, computational physics and closed-loop control, I've used each project as an opportunity to learn something new and take on a more challenging problem.
